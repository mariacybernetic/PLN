US Envoy Jared Kushner says Doha attack left Israel 'globally isolated'
The US Special Envoy for Peace, Jared Kushner, says Israel's failed attack on Hamas leadership in Doha was 'terrible' and left the country 'globally isolated'. He added that this allowed US mediators to use international outrage to force Israel into a Gaza deal.
